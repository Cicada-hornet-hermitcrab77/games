Everyone starts with Brawler, Boxer, Ninja and Phantom, and most people
never think about them again. They are worth a second look, because almost
every early unlock asks you to win matches, and these are the four you have
to win them with.

## What each one is for
- Brawler takes a beating. Use him while you are still learning to block.
- Boxer punches fastest. Short reach, so you have to walk in.
- Ninja double jumps. The platforms on most maps belong to him.
- Phantom is the awkward one. Good when you already know what you are doing.

## A rough order to unlock in
Win two matches with anyone and Ares turns up. Three more and Zephyr does.
Nothing early asks for a particular fighter, so use whichever of the four
you win most with, and come back for the fussy unlocks later.

Matches to first unlock = 2
Fighters you start with = 4
